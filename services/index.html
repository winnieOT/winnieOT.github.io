Services & Fees

Every new client receives a free 30-minute consultation (by phone or video call) to discuss your needs, ask questions, and explore whether OT is the right fit — before any commitment or cost.

What We Offer

Services

We provide a range of occupational therapy services for adults and children, in the community or at home.

Virtual OT

Comprehensive evaluation of daily living abilities

A thorough assessment of your ability to carry out everyday activities such as self-care, meal preparation, mobility, and community access. Assessments inform individualised treatment plans and, where relevant, professional reports for funding, legal, or insurance purposes.

Home Environment Assessment

Evaluating your home for safety and adaptations

A visit to your home to assess safety, accessibility, and independence. We identify potential hazards, recommend adaptations (such as grab rails, ramps, or stair lifts), and liaise with local authorities or contractors on your behalf where required.

Mental Health OT

Support for wellbeing, routines, and daily function

Targeted support for individuals experiencing the impact of mental health conditions on everyday functioning. Sessions focus on building structured routines, developing coping strategies, improving self-care, and rebuilding confidence in meaningful activities.

Practicalities

Payment & Arranging Appointments

All appointments are arranged directly with the occupational therapist by email. There is no online booking system.

Once you have made contact and we have completed the free initial consultation, we will agree a time and method for your assessment or session that works best for you.

  • Payment is due at the time of the appointment unless otherwise agreed
  • Bank transfer and other payment methods are accepted — details provided on invoice
  • Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice may incur a cancellation fee
  • Invoices and receipts provided for all sessions — suitable for insurance claims
