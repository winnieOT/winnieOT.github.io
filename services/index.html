Services & Fees

Occupational Therapy Services

Virtual Sessions

Treatment sessions are 60 minutes.

After 60 minutes, sessions are pro-rated at 15 minute intervals.

$120 / session

In-Person Sessions

Available in Ottawa.

Rate varies based on travel time and location within the Ottawa area.

After 60 minutes, sessions are pro-rated at 15 minute intervals.

Services are provided in your home or pre-arranged community space that meets your needs for privacy and confidentiality.

$150-$180 / session

Consultative Services

Consultative services are offered to professionals or caregivers looking to better support a group or individual.

A written summary of recommendations is included.

$180 / session

Getting Started

Here's a map to show you how to get started and what to expect.

Step 1 — Get in Touch

Tell us a bit about yourself, and book a 20-minute complimentary consultation to start the conversation.

Step 2 — Complimentary Consultation

We’ll meet virtually to see if Everest Therapy is a good fit for you.

If you’d like to move forward, we’ll book an assessment.

Step 3 — Assessment

Assessments are typically 90 minutes long.

We’ll start with your story and ask detailed questions to learn about you. We’ll set goals together, and prioritize what to work on first in treatment.

Step 4 — Treatment

We’ll spend treatment sessions checking in, working on your goals, and helping you grow your toolbox. You will often get strategies to work on between sessions.

Additional Fees Information

Occupational Therapy services are tax exempt in Ontario, but are not covered by the Ontario Health Insurance Plan (OHIP). Occupational Therapy services may be claimed as a medical expense for income tax purposes. If you have extended health care benefits, some plans may have coverage for Occupational Therapy services. Please check with your benefits provider regarding your coverage eligibility.
