About Everest Therapy

What is Occupational Therapy?

Occupational Therapy (OT) focuses on functional skill building to help you manage everyday life. You may be dealing with low mood, anxiety, ADHD or other worries affecting you. Your goal might be to find healthy coping strategies, improve your well-being, or to get out of survival mode. Together, we work to strengthen your skills and build up a toolbox to help you navigate whatever comes your way.

OT begins with your story. We provide supportive counselling to give you a safe space to express your thoughts and emotions. Then we use an evidence-based clinical approach to give you strategies to tackle your challenges with confidence. We work together to achieve your goals and get you back to living.

Areas of Support

Every story is different. Here are some of the challenges we can work on together. You might see yourself in one of these, or somewhere in between.

Neurodivergence and Executive Function

  • ADHD or neurodivergence with difficulty starting and finishing tasks, time management and routines
  • Adult ADHD diagnosis and wanting strategies to manage this diagnosis
  • Executive function dysregulation leading to a gap between goals and current reality

Mood Regulation and Coping

  • Depression or low mood with low energy and low motivation
  • Feeling anxious and overwhelmed or being unable to relax
  • School- or work-related stress and burn out

Social Connection and Transitions

  • Navigating loneliness, practicing social skills, or strengthening current relationships
  • Experiencing a big life transition with trouble adjusting
  • Struggling with school, work, or job searching

Is Everest Therapy right for me?

At Everest Therapy, we see you before the diagnosis and the letters. We want to hear your story, and we will be working together every step of the way to enable you to reach your goals. We will face the mountain together, and then we will give you the gear you need to reach the top.

Our commitments at Everest Therapy:

  • Neuro-affirming care
  • Strengths-based approach
  • Holding space for safe expression and confidentiality
  • Value diversity and inclusion with compassion for every walk of life
  • Therapeutic partnership using a collaborative approach
  • Person-first care with individualized strategies
