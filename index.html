Your toolbox for everyday life.

We will build the skills toolbox together so that it’s meant for you.

Task Initiation Strategies

We will identify causes for procrastination and difficulty with initiation, and build strategies to address the barriers and reduce overwhelm.

Emotional Regulation Skills

We will help you process events, situations, triggers, and emotions. We will find strategies for you to regulate your emotions that would work for you.

Time Management Skills

We will set up internal or external aids to increase time awareness, and work towards setting realistic time goals.

Increase Self-Compassion

We will increase awareness of any automatic negative thoughts, process the contents of negative self-talk, and restructure your relationship with you.

Sleep Hygiene Routines

We will discuss your sleep goals, consider your current nighttime routine and modify it to support your sleep.

Work on a Resume and Job Applications

We will work on resume writing and practice practical skills towards job seeking.

Social Skills and Connections

We will help you strengthen your social skills, communication skills, and help you build connections.
